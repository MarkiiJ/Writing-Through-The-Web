HEADLINES AND HOOKS

  • HEADLINE (TITLE TAG / H1): SHOULD BE CLEAR, SPECIFIC, AND HONEST[cite: 4].
  • INVERTED PYRAMID: PUT THE MOST IMPORTANT INFORMATION FIRST, THEN SUPPORTING DETAILS, THEN BACKGROUND[cite: 4].
  • LEAD: THE OPENING SENTENCE OR PARAGRAPH THAT STATES THE MAIN POINT[cite: 4].
  • CLICKBAIT: A MISLEADING HEADLINE THAT PROMISES MORE THAN THE CONTENT DELIVERS. AVOID IT[cite: 4].
